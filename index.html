01 / BUILD

PROFESSION 01 • TECHNOLOGY

Software
Developer

Software developers create the programs people use on computers, phones, and other devices.

Main responsibilities

They plan features, write and improve code, test their work, and fix problems. They often work with designers and other developers.

Logical thinkingCreativityTeamwork

Why it matters: Their work helps people communicate, learn, work, and have fun.

✦
02 / DESIGN

PROFESSION 02 • DESIGN

UX/UI
Designer

A UX/UI designer plans how a website or app looks and how easy it is for people to use.

Main responsibilities

They sketch ideas, choose colors and fonts, arrange buttons and menus, and ask users for feedback before improving a design.

CreativityEmpathyCommunication

Why it matters: Good design helps people find information without getting confused.

⌕
03 / ANALYZE

PROFESSION 03 • RESEARCH

Data
Analyst

A data analyst collects and studies information to understand what is happening and why.

Main responsibilities

They check information for mistakes, organize numbers, create charts, and explain their findings to a team or a business.

MathematicsCuriosityAccuracy

Why it matters: Clear data can help schools, hospitals, and companies make better decisions.

⌁
04 / PROTECT
✓

PROFESSION 04 • SAFETY

Cybersecurity
Specialist

A cybersecurity specialist helps protect people and organizations from online dangers.

Main responsibilities

They check systems for weaknesses, monitor suspicious activity, improve security rules, and respond to possible attacks.

AttentionProblem-solvingResponsibility

Why it matters: Their work helps protect private messages, money, and personal information.

05 / TEST
TEST REPORT✓ Passed✓ Passed× Failed

PROFESSION 05 • QUALITY

Quality
Assurance Tester

A quality assurance tester checks a program to make sure it works as expected.

Main responsibilities

They try different actions, look for errors, write bug reports, and test fixes to see if a problem has been solved.

PatienceAccuracyClear writing

Why it matters: Testing helps make apps, games, and websites more reliable.

⌁
06 / CONNECT

PROFESSION 06 • COMMUNICATION

Network
Engineer

A network engineer makes it possible for computers and devices to share information.

Main responsibilities

They install and maintain routers, switches, cables, and wireless networks. They also solve connection problems and help keep networks reliable.

Technical skillsLogicTeamwork

Why it matters: Reliable networks help people study, work, and communicate online.

☁
07 / CLOUD

PROFESSION 07 • ONLINE SERVICES

Cloud
Engineer

A cloud engineer works with computing services that run over the internet instead of only on one computer.

Main responsibilities

They set up online servers, storage, and tools. They help services stay available, protect information, and handle changes in demand.

PlanningOrganizationProblem-solving

Why it matters: Cloud systems let people use websites and apps from many places.

⌨
08 / SUPPORT
?✓!

PROFESSION 08 • CUSTOMER SERVICE

IT Support
Specialist

An IT support specialist helps people when their computers or digital tools do not work properly.

Main responsibilities

They answer questions, install software, solve common technical problems, and explain solutions in a clear way.

CommunicationPatiencePractical skills

Why it matters: Their help allows people to return to work or study more quickly.

▣
09 / AUTOMATE

PROFESSION 09 • DELIVERY

DevOps
Engineer

A DevOps engineer helps software teams build, test, and release updates more smoothly.

Main responsibilities

They automate repeated tasks, manage deployment tools, monitor systems, and work with developers to solve problems quickly.

AutomationTeamworkOrganization

Why it matters: Teams can deliver useful updates more often and with fewer mistakes.

✧
10 / INTELLIGENCE
AI+

PROFESSION 10 • NEW TECHNOLOGY

AI / Machine
Learning Engineer

An AI or machine learning engineer develops computer systems that find patterns in data and use them to make predictions or complete tasks.

Main responsibilities

They prepare data, train and test models, measure their results, and improve how an AI system performs.

MathematicsProgrammingCuriosity

Why it matters: AI can support translation, accessibility, research, and many everyday services.
